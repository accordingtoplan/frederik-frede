Digital · Transformation

"A digital agency moves inside a network agency."

In 2010 TBWA acquired NoMoreSleep for its Berlin Digital Arts group. The brief was to make the whole agency digital, not to add a digital unit next to it.

NoMoreSleep started in Berlin in 2006. A digital agency and production studio. One of its builds was Iconist, an iPad-only magazine for the Welt group at Axel Springer.

Four years in, TBWA acquired the agency.

01 — Context

Where digital sat in 2010.

Network agencies in Germany were deciding how to handle digital. Some kept it in separate sister companies. TBWA ran a digital-only agency in other markets and chose not to open it in Germany.

TBWA Germany went the other way. The plan was to digitise the whole agency, starting in Berlin.

02 — The deal

An acquisition, ten people.

In August 2010 TBWA acquired NoMoreSleep. Ten people moved over to TBWA Berlin, the founders among them.

The team joined the Digital Arts group. Terms were not made public.

03 — The transformation

Digital inside the core agency.

Digital Arts sat inside the core agency, not next to it. After the move, about a fifth of the roughly 80 people in the Berlin office had a digital background. Berlin was set up as the model for the Hamburg and Düsseldorf offices to follow.

04 — Outcomes

Then a new studio.

MoreSleep opened in Berlin in 2012, with a model built around remote work and sustainable hours.

In 2010 TBWA acquired NoMoreSleep and brought its team into the Berlin Digital Arts group. TBWA Germany planned Berlin as the model for building digital into the core agency.

MoreSleep followed in 2012.
