Roots Management

Brand · Digital

"Where grit meets community."

Brand identity and website design for ROOTS Management, a Berlin-based sports and music agency focused on racing, events, and creative culture.

ROOTS is built around the collision of racing, events, and sports marketing. The brand needed a digital presence that matched that energy without softening it.

The approach centred on dynamic video content and active lifestyle imagery. A new website showcases their events and community in a layout that prioritises motion and visual impact.

01 — Digital Identity

Built for the speed of the culture.

A flexible visual identity anchored in ROOTS' gritty aesthetic. High-impact visuals were developed to sit across web, social, and event collateral.

02 — Website

A community hub, not just a portfolio.

Designed to feel as dynamic as the brand it represents, with interactive features and hover states that reflect the energy of the events they run.
