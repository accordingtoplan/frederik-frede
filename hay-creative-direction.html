HAY

Creative Direction

"Good design for everyday life."

Creative direction for HAY's Milan Design Week presence, positioning the Danish design brand within the global conversation on contemporary furniture and living.

HAY is one of the most influential Danish design brands of the last two decades, building a following around the belief that good design should be accessible for everyday life. We worked with them on their Milan Design Week presence and creative direction.

The brief was to place HAY within the broader conversation happening across Milan that week, showing products while communicating a point of view on how people live and what they value.
