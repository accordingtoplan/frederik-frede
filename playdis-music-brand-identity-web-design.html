Playdis

Identity · Digital

"A visual identity and digital home for a musical company."

Visual identity, art direction and website for Playdis, composers of original music for film and advertising.

Playdis works with world-class talent on original compositions. Independent short films, international commercials.

The brief: a modern identity and a new website to show the work around the globe.

01 — Identity

Where sound meets image.

Geometric and organic lines meet in the mark. A picture of audio immersed in video.

The aesthetic is refined and bespoke. Made to set Playdis apart and to carry the quality of the work.

02 — Website

The work comes first.

The redesign puts the picture, and with it the sound, of each project in front. Animations and interactions guide visitors through the site.

Colour from the work runs through the interface. A visual language with some musicality to it.

Visual identity, art direction and website for Playdis. The range and colour of the work now sit at the core of the brand.
