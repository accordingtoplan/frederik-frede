MINI

Strategy · Campaign · Editorial · Event

"Exploring the future of urban living."

Brand identity, platform, podcast and events for The Sooner Now, plus a brand lookbook and the Clubman campaign for MINI Germany.

The Sooner Now is a joint initiative of MINI Germany and Freunde von Freunden. From 2016 it brought creatives together to explore and question the future of urban living.

The series started with 300 people at FvF Friends Space in Berlin and travelled to six cities in Germany. Two more projects for MINI sit alongside it: a lookbook on the brand DNA and a national campaign for the MINI Clubman.

01 — The Sooner Now: Identity

Old and new, merged into something forward.

All branding and collateral for The Sooner Now. Posters, programmes and invitations move at the pace of city streets. The logo and type mash up serif and sans-serif styles into new letterforms.

02 — Platform, Podcast and Social

A platform that keeps the ideas circulating.

The events led to an online platform that keeps the ideas circulating and growing, edited by Freunde von Freunden. The Sooner Now became a monthly podcast on alternative economies, mobility, urban space and the social development of culture.

Social content and channel management ran alongside.

03 — The Sooner Now: Events

Conversations worth having, in cities worth knowing.

From 2016 the series travelled to cities across Germany, gathering people from art, design, architecture, gastronomy and sustainability. In Berlin in 2019 a MINI Electric stood in a courtyard taped in neon yellow. Photography by Robert Rieger.

04 — The Sooner Now: Film

The series in motion.

A save-the-date teaser, a film on improving the city of today, and recaps from Berlin and the 2018 tour.

05 — MINI Lookbook

Mapping the living worlds of a brand.

A coffee-table book on the Lebenswelten, the living worlds, of the new MINI brand DNA. History, style and everyday use, told in spreads that mix archive, studio and street photography.

06 — MINI Clubman Campaign

Kombi. Aus nützlich und will ich.

National campaign for the MINI Clubman, launched in summer 2016. A TV spot and an online film pair the car with a young Berlin furniture label and a New Zealand indie band. Both combine a sense for the practical with a love of the beautiful, like the car.

The spot below plays from a third-party upload. Campaign announcement: BMW Group PressClub, “Kombi. Aus nützlich und will ich.” Neue Kampagne von MINI Deutschland für den MINI Clubman startet, 2016.

What will cities look and feel like in the near future?

MINI Germany asked a big question: what will cities look and feel like in the near future? The Sooner Now answered with a brand, an online platform, a podcast and an event series across Germany, built with Freunde von Freunden.

A lookbook on the MINI brand DNA and a national campaign for the MINI Clubman complete the work for MINI.
