Mizzuni

Brand · E-Commerce · Web Design

"Furniture, made personal."

Brand identity and e-commerce website for Mizzuni, a furniture platform offering over 3,000 customisable products from selected EU-based manufacturers.

Mizzuni brings sustainable design to individual and professional living spaces, combining decades of experience in interior design with an online platform built for customisation.

A new visual identity, logo, warm colour palette, hand-drawn illustrations, gave the brand a welcoming, approachable character. The e-commerce experience was built to put the products front and centre.

01 — Brand Identity

Quality and warmth in equal measure.

New logo embodying quality and elegance. Subtle warm palette. Hand-drawn illustrations adding a friendly, human character to the identity system.

02 — E-Commerce

Expertise meets endless customisation.

Products shown in multiple configurations. An advanced search module handles complex filtering across categories. An image-map feature lets users drag to reveal products in real environments.

More boutique than browse.

The site serves both consumers and design professionals, with the same warmth and care across both experiences.
