A company's voice, rules, facts and decisions in one system. Every person and every AI tool works from it, and it grows with every piece of work.

A brand lives in many hands. Marketing, sales, agencies, and now AI tools. Each works from its own copy: an old deck, a PDF, a quick prompt. Over time the brand says different things in different places. Nobody gets it wrong on purpose. There is simply no single version to work from.

The idea

A gentle system.

It works in the background and supports the people who carry the brand and the business. It drafts, checks and remembers. The decision stays with the team.

In the background

Inside the tools the team already uses. Nothing new to learn.

People decide

It drafts and asks. The call stays with the people who own the brand.

One company, every voice

Whoever writes, and whatever tool they use, it reads as the same company.

Quiet when it is right

It speaks up only where a rule is broken or missing, or where it sees a chance to improve.

01 · Why

Run the company on what it already knows.

One center

Every business, office, agency and tool works from one source, held in one place.

Lower cost

Less agency time on first drafts and fixes. Less rework when something goes out in the wrong words.

Faster turnaround

A first draft in the company's voice in minutes, checked against the rules before it reaches review.

It learns

Every decision made on a piece of work is written back. Next month the company knows more than it did this month.

It stays private

On the company's own servers, or in its own cloud account. The files belong to the company, whatever the vendor.

Fewer hires

The system carries the repeatable work. The same team does more. Headcount stays where it is.

02 · How it works

Four steps. One rhythm.

01

Written down

The brand and the company's rules as plain files. Each rule carries its reason, in the client's own words, with a date. Logos, colours and fonts are stored with them.

02

Read by every tool

People read the files. AI tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Copilot read them before they write. One source behind all of them, including a private AI workspace on the company's own servers.

03

Checked

New drafts are read against the files before they go out, and so is what is already live: sites, decks, listings. Whatever the rules do not cover comes back as a question.

04

Written back

Decisions, feedback and who decides what go into the files the same day. The AI suggests. People decide. That is how the company learns.

Why not hand an AI tool the brand PDF? A PDF shows what was decided. The system also carries why, where each rule applies and what to do when none does. Then it checks the work and keeps every new decision.

03 · Strategy and planning

The thinking comes first.

Before anything is written, the system holds where the company stands and where it is going. Strategy and plans are made from the same facts as everything else.

01

Positioning

Where the company stands, for whom, and what sets it apart. Written down with the reasons, so every team argues from the same place.

02

Plans

Launches, campaigns and the year ahead, drafted from current figures and past results. Ready for review within days.

03

Briefs

Every brief starts from the system: the goal, the audience, the facts and the rules that apply. Agencies get the same brief as the team.

04

The other side

Before a recommendation goes to the board, the system builds the strongest case against it. The objection arrives early, in private.

Everyone has the same AI.
The difference is what it knows about your company.

Case 01 · A brand PDF against a brand system

One question on a Tuesday afternoon.

A sales manager needs a poster for a partner's event by tomorrow. Which logo, which colour, which line about the company?

The brand PDF

Somewhere on page 40.

Dozens of pages, read once and then searched. It shows what was decided, never why, and says nothing about a case nobody drew. Out of date the day it was exported. The poster goes out with a guess.

The brand system

An answer in Slack, in seconds.

Asked in Slack, Teams or a direct message. The brand system replies with the rule, the reason and the right file, and checks the draft before it goes out. When the answer is a new decision, it goes back in the same day.
