"A digital home for the best sunshade solutions."

Website and interactive product advisor for Schattenfinder, a sunshade platform built with leading German manufacturers.

Schattenfinder helps homeowners find the right sunshade. An online platform launched with industry-leading German brands, together with an interactive recommendation questionnaire.

01 — Platform

Everything in one place.

Over 100 customisable solutions from five manufacturers. The backend runs on SPOT, a custom asset management system. All product data and filter logic come from one data source.

Landing pages and questionnaire share the same flexible structure.

02 — Product advisor

Not sure which solution fits?

An interactive advisor points users to the right categories and products. A short quiz asks about requirements and mounting options.

Two parts allow very granular filtering. Everything stays in sync with the central data in SPOT.

03 — Powered by SPOT

Asset management, simplified.

SPOT was built by MoreSleep and ENGN. A leaner, more affordable answer to the cost and complexity of asset management systems. Files stay together, current and easy to check.
