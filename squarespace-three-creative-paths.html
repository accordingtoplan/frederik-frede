Three Creative Paths: from passion project to career.

Three Creative Paths: a Friends of Friends film and editorial series for Squarespace with three Berlin founders who built a career online, 2022.

Squarespace sells websites. The series looked at what a website does for people who turn something they love into a living.

Friends of Friends met three Berlin founders in spring 2022: a photography collective, a city blog and a meal-delivery service. Each one got a short film and a photographed interview. A fourth film brings the three together.

01 — The Journal Collective

A visual record by women and non-binary photographers.

In March 2020 photographer Charlotte Schmitz asked who would document a moment that was changing daily life everywhere. The answer became The Journal Collective, an online platform for women and non-binary photographers to share personal visual stories.

02 — Stil in Berlin

A blog that became a job.

Mary Scherpe co-founded Stil in Berlin in 2006 to photograph street style in a city she had just moved to. The blog grew into an internationally read guide to local, independent culture, food and travel.

03 — Personal Chef Berlin

Home cooking, delivered weekly.

Self-taught cook Chi You started Personal Chef Berlin in 2020, a weekly meal-prep service for healthy home-cooked food. A clear online presence lets her spend her time in the kitchen.
