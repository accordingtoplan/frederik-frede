Brands, built with AI in the room.

Every engagement now runs with AI as a working partner. Not a production shortcut. A thinking tool, present from the first brief.

Umane shows the method. Strategy, naming, identity and a full design system, developed in conversation with Claude and pressure-tested against the brand's own logic in real time. Delivered in two layers: guidelines for people, a structured system of YAML and Markdown for agents. Same brand, two formats.

The setup has a name: Directive Intelligence. A digital mind. Thirty years of standards, references and voice, written down so a machine can follow them. It drafts, compares and argues the way the practice does.

Tools are chosen per task, never the other way around. Claude and GPT in combination, each where it is strongest. Figma carries the design work. Higgsfield, Reve and After Effects carry image and motion. The framework decides what runs where.

The consulting side works the other way around. Companies bring questions about what AI means for their brand, their content and their teams. The answers arrive as working prototypes, not slide decks.
