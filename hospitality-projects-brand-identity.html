Hospitality Projects

Identity · Digital

Identity for a local, global and cool hospitality mindset.

Brand identity and digital presence for Hospitality Projects, the Tbilisi-based group widely credited with shaping Georgia's rise as a global cultural and tourism destination.

Valeri Chekheria and Levan Berulava are hospitality pioneers behind Georgia's rapid rise over the past decade as a global cultural hub and travel destination. The brief was a brand identity and digital presence reflecting a contemporary, forward-thinking and innovative way of working.

The mark at the centre of the identity encapsulates a sun, its rays resonating outward as a representation of the premium quality and warmth Hospitality Projects brings to its guests and partners.

01 — Identity & Digital

A symbol built to inspire.

The brief was to reflect a key value: inspiring others to keep beautiful journeys going, and to breathe new life into a hospitality sector heavily impacted by the pandemic.

An identity that travels as far as the brand it represents.

Brand identity and digital presence for Hospitality Projects, distilling a local, global and cool mindset into a single radiant mark and a calm, confident digital presence.

An identity built to scale across hospitality ventures while keeping a sense of warmth and place at the centre.
