NOMOS Glashütte

Editorial · Film

"Nomos at Work."

Nomos at Work, a Friends of Friends series for NOMOS Glashütte on the changing nature of work, with portraits and films in Berlin, New York and San Francisco.

NOMOS Glashütte builds mechanical watches in a small town in Saxony. Nomos at Work looked at a question outside the watch world: what does work mean today, and what will it mean tomorrow? Each story asked about success, vision and status, told from the places where people work and live.

Friends of Friends developed the editorial concept and led the research. FvF Productions sourced the protagonists from its network and developed the video narrative. Three portraits in three cities, two of them also as films.

01 — Berlin · 2017

New work, on a small scale.

Ansgar Oberholz opened St. Oberholz at Rosenthaler Platz in 2005. It was the first cafe in Berlin built around freelancers, founders and their laptops. SoundCloud and HelloFresh started at its tables. He later co-founded the Institut für Neue Arbeit and advises companies on how they work.

The film and the photo story follow him through Mitte. The cafe and its coworking floors, a quiet garrison cemetery, a fab lab and the office of Moebel Horzon.

02 — New York · 2017–2018

A home worth coming back to.

Tariq Dixon co-founded TRNK, an online shop for home design, with Nick Nemechek. Both had worked as menswear buyers. Their showroom in NoHo is set up like an apartment and doubles as the office.

The episode was shot in New York. The crew filmed at his Brooklyn apartment, the showroom and the studio of a ceramicist who makes pieces for TRNK. The photo story ran in November 2017, the film followed in February 2018.

03 — San Francisco · 2017

Just strategy.

Michael Martin is a managing partner at Code and Theory and leads its West Coast office. The agency designs publishing platforms for media companies such as Bloomberg, The Verge and the BBC.

The San Francisco portrait ran as a photo story. It moves from his apartment in Jackson Square through Chinatown to the agency office on Maiden Lane.
