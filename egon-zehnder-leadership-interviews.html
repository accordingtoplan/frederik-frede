"Portrait interviews with leadership thinkers."

A pair of filmed interviews for executive search firm Egon Zehnder, exploring new models of leadership and organisational change.

For Egon Zehnder, Friends of Friends produced a series of filmed interviews with leadership thinkers exploring how organisations are rethinking management, hierarchy and culture.

The first, with author and consultant Frédéric Laloux, explores the ideas behind his bestselling book “Reinventing Organizations” and why the future belongs to self-managing teams. A second interview features Erica Ariel Fox on navigating leadership transitions.

Sense and respond, not predict and control.

Two portrait interviews produced for Egon Zehnder's editorial platform, filmed on location with leadership thinkers Frédéric Laloux and Erica Ariel Fox.

Each film pairs a quiet, observational visual style with conversation, translating Egon Zehnder's executive-search expertise into accessible editorial content.
