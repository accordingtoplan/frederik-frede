ART+TECH Report

Brand · Digital · Publishing

"Where art meets technology."

Design system and website for the ART+TECH Report 2023, presenting data at the intersection of art and technology through dynamic visualisations and fluid transitions.

The ART+TECH Report tracks the convergence of creative practice and emerging technology. The 2023 edition needed a design system and website that could match the ambition of its content.

A new design system was built around dynamic data visualisations, with a refreshed aesthetic and smooth animations. The result is a publication that reads as fluidly as it looks.

01 — Design System

Data made legible, not just visible.

The visual language was rebuilt to handle complex data sets across responsive contexts, prioritising clarity and motion over decoration.

02 — Website

A platform as forward-thinking as its subject.

Developed with fluid transitions and interactive elements across desktop and mobile, making the report's content as engaging to navigate as it is to read.
