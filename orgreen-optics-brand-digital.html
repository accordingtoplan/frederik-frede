Brand & Digital

"Scandinavian eyewear through a fresh lens."

Brand refresh and digital presence for Ørgreen Optics: Danish handcrafted eyewear built on colour, craft, and a distinctly Scandinavian sensibility.

Ørgreen is a Danish eyewear company innovating Scandinavian design with sophisticated yet playful shapes and futuristic materials.

We created a digital strategy and a new website that reflects Ørgreen's pursuit of originality. Utilising design in a forward-thinking and contemporary way, Ørgreen now has a fresh online experience tailored to the needs and behaviours of consumers and opticians.

01 — Digital Strategy

A timelessly simple, fully immersive experience.

Fluid transitions and effects create a smooth user experience, while soft interactions make shopping for eyewear more intuitive and pleasurable.

02 — Editorial Design

Colour and content in conversation.

Colour enhances content to create expressive and contemporary pages. Products are given an editorial feel and users can browse magazine-style features and interviews that go deeper into the Ørgreen brand.

A website as original and progressive as the eyewear itself.

Ørgreen Optics is a Danish eyewear brand built on the belief that glasses should be considered objects: handcrafted, colourful, and deeply personal. They came to us for a brand refresh and new digital home.

We positioned Ørgreen around their founding conviction: colour is craft. The digital presence reflects the brand's Scandinavian restraint while giving their extraordinary frames the spotlight they deserve.
