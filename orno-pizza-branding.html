ORNO

Identity · Digital

"Communicating the fire and passion of Argentine pizza."

Branding and web design for ORNO, a Buenos Aires pizzeria from Mezcla Gastronomía reinterpreting Neapolitan pizza with a Miami Art Deco edge.

ORNO is a restaurant from Argentina's Mezcla Gastronomía that interprets classic Neapolitan pizza using the finest local ingredients. At its heart is the Forni Grimaldi, a 400-degree traditional pizza oven, with every pie made in full view of guests.

The interior mixes modernist shapes in vibrant pastels, a chic update on Miami Art Deco that's the opposite of a traditional pizzeria. Based on a sharing concept, the oven takes centre stage and the entire space echoes its basic geometry.

The heat of the oven, at the heart of the brand.

The identity uses geometric, basic shapes with a contrasting red logo that symbolises ORNO's core: the heat of their pizza oven. Working closely with the Mezcla team, the studio ideated and produced all collateral in the space, from exterior signage to stickers to pizza boxes.

The website follows the same look and feel: a simple one-page design with easy navigation, strong photography and insight into the local producers behind ORNO's pizza.
