Selfnation

Strategy · Editorial

"A campaign tailor-made for the best fitting jeans."

An editorial TV and online campaign for Selfnation, a high-tech sustainable jeans and chinos brand, built around the tagline “The Perfect Fit.”

Selfnation is a high-tech jeans and chinos brand founded by a mechanical engineer and an information technologist. Their quest to find well-fitting, high-quality denim led them to start a sustainable fashion company of their own — the studio had to let people know.

MoreSleep spearheaded an editorially-driven TV and online campaign for Selfnation, with a wide variety of touchpoints and engagement levels all driving traffic directly to the point of sale.

The Perfect Fit.

A 20-second TV spot, broadcast in Germany, Austria and Switzerland, captured the attention to detail, materials and quality that make Selfnation stand out. A longer, documentary-style cut was distributed on YouTube via Google TrueView.

The campaign was followed by a social media activation around the tagline “The Perfect Fit” and an online banner campaign to get the message out.
