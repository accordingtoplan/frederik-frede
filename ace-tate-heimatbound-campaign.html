Heimatbound: portraits of a city chosen as home.

An editorial portrait series for Ace & Tate, exploring why young creatives chose Berlin as the place to build their careers.

A joint series with Ace & Tate produced portraits of young, talented creatives who followed Berlin's pull as a creative haven, centred on the conflicting nature of relocation and what the city holds for each of them.

Four portraits featured musician Mikey Woodbridge, illustrator Ruohan Wang, Berlin Feminist Film Week founder Karin Fornander and DJ Nico Adomako, each photographed in their own choice of Ace & Tate frames.

01 — A Collaborative Collection

From portrait to product.

Each subject's choice of frames became a collaborative collection featured on Ace & Tate's online store. Services spanned talent research, content concept, creative direction, four editorial interview productions, guest management, event production, printed collateral and photography documentation.
