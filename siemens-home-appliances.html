Siemens

Brand Partnership · Editorial · Film Production

Home appliances, kitchens and the people who live with them.

A long-running content partnership with Siemens Home Appliances. Editorial film and interview series produced across European and global cities, from chefs in their kitchens to architects in self-built homes.

Over several years, Siemens Home Appliances became a long-standing content partner. The work moved between two editorial series and a shared physical space, each built on the same idea: that products are best understood inside real homes and real lives.

What started as a handful of Berlin home portraits grew into an international series, running alongside a kitchen-focused film series with a new generation of European chefs.

01 — Culinary Encounters

Four kitchens, four cities.

Culinary Encounters stepped into the kitchens of four up-and-coming chefs across Oslo, Paris, Berlin and Maastricht, examining how a new generation approaches food, kitchen culture and recipe development. Each chef was filmed and interviewed in their own kitchen, with Siemens Home Appliances integrated into the everyday rituals of preparation, plating and conversation.

A new generation, cooking on its own terms.
02 — Home Stories

Innovative living, in real homes.

Home Stories explored innovative urban living through the people who shape it: architects, gallerists and makers in unusual homes across global cities. It began with three Berlin portraits and extended to Stockholm, London, Hong Kong, Antwerp, Melbourne and the Dutch countryside. Filmed on location with cinematographer Shai Levy, each portrait paired a documentary film with a long-form interview.

The home as a window into how people really live.
03 — Friends Space

A kitchen built to bring people together.

The partnership extended into a shared physical space. The Friends Space kitchen in Berlin's Kreuzberg was a collaboration with Siemens Home Appliances: an open design with state-of-the-art appliances at its centre, built to host dinners, gatherings and conversations within the wider network.

A multi-year content partnership with Siemens Home Appliances, spanning two editorial film series and a shared physical space.

Documentary portraits connecting the brand's products to the everyday practice of chefs, architects and makers, produced across European and global cities.
