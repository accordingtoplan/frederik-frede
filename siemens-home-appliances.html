Siemens

Editorial · Film

Home appliances, kitchens and the people who live with them.

2015–2019 content partnership: home films, chef portraits, architect interviews and a Siemens kitchen in the Friends Space, produced by Friends of Friends across Europe, Asia and Australia.

Siemens Home Appliances sells to a mass market. Its ovens and fridges are reliable and easy to overlook. A younger, design-minded audience rarely thinks about the brand until the day it plans a kitchen. The partnership set out to reach that audience earlier, inside the homes and kitchens it already admired.

Friends of Friends produced the work from 2015 to 2019. It began with three home portraits in Berlin and grew into four formats: documentary home films in seven cities, a chef series in four, an interview series with architects, and a Siemens kitchen built into the Friends Space in Kreuzberg. The films ran on Friends of Friends and on Siemens channels. FRAME and Baumeister carried select interviews.

01 — Home Stories · 2015–2018

Innovative living, in real homes.

Home Stories looked at urban living through the people who shape it: architects, gallerists and makers in unusual homes. Each film shows the space and the life inside it, kitchen included. The series opened in 2015 with three Berlin portraits: a former lingerie factory, a houseboat and a 1950s gas station. It travelled on to London, Stockholm, Antwerp, Melbourne, Hong Kong and the Dutch countryside until 2018.

02 — Culinary Encounters · 2019

Four kitchens, four cities.

Culinary Encounters followed four young chefs in Oslo, Paris, Berlin and Maastricht and asked how a new generation approaches food, kitchen culture and new recipes. Each chef was filmed and interviewed in their own city and kitchen. Filmed in 2018.

The series ran in two parts. The home portraits ran on Friends of Friends from February to April 2019. The kitchen films brought each chef to the Friends Space in Berlin to cook one dish in the Siemens kitchen, and ran on Siemens channels from March 2019.

03 — Architect Dialogues · 2019

Design philosophies, four cities.

Architect Dialogues was an interview series with award-winning architects and interior designers in Berlin, Paris, Amsterdam and Helsinki. Each conversation took place in their homes or studios and covered design philosophy, urban living and the challenges facing cities. Extended versions ran on the Siemens Home Appliances site. FRAME and Baumeister carried select interviews to their readers.

04 — Built-in Kitchen · 2016–2018

A kitchen built to bring people together.

The Friends Space is the physical home of Friends of Friends: a 350 sqm loft in Kreuzberg, designed with architect Philipp Mainzer and opened in 2016. Its kitchen is a collaboration with Siemens Home Appliances. Black built-in units, an induction hob with integrated extraction and an open layout make it work for cooking classes, dinner presentations and film shoots.

The kitchen came out of the Built-in Kitchen campaign for Siemens. Four creatives from four countries shaped it: designers Anna-Riika and Katriina Nuutinen in Helsinki, chef Jos Timmer in Amsterdam, photographer Fabrice Fouillet in Paris and kitchen designer Andrea Balonier in Berlin. Portrait films show each of them at home, talking about the kitchen they need. The case film follows the process from sketches and planning to factory visits and installation in the Friends Space.

The kitchen served as a set for the partnership. The Culinary Encounters chefs cooked their dishes there for the kitchen films. It also hosted the wider network: supper clubs such as Clam Club, brand dinners for up to 120 guests, and productions for other clients.
