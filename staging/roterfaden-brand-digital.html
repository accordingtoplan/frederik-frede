"Digital craft for handmade objects."

Brand identity refresh and website for Roterfaden, an independent German maker of premium handcrafted personal planners.

ROTERFADEN makes premium handcrafted personal organisers, the Taschenbegleiter, in Germany. 100% independent. The brief was to build an online experience as beautiful and well-considered as the products themselves.

The website was designed from a user-first perspective: highly visual, curated, more boutique than shop. The homepage makes space for the brand to breathe, with dedicated storytelling pages covering values and sustainability.
