02 — The other way round

Direction Intel exists because a working practice needed it.

Every skill in it has run on live projects, under real deadlines, for real clients. The method case study on this site documents that work as it happens. What remains when the work is done is the intel: the tools and the judgment behind them, extracted and made usable by someone else.

03 — What's inside

Around ten deep skills. Not forty shallow ones.

Each one encodes a way of working that took years to earn. Plain language throughout. No dashboards, no personas, no setup theater.
