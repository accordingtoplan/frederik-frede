Judgment, held under deadline.

A working system for creative directors and strategists. Around ten deep skills, built from thirty years of practice and proven on real client work before anything ships.

Every week another kit promises to automate creative work. Forty skills, set up in five minutes, an AI team with headshots. The machinery is real and the machinery is cheap. What none of it carries is the part clients actually pay for: the ability to hold a judgment when the deadline is tomorrow and the room disagrees.

Direction Intel is built the other way round. The system exists because a working practice needed it. Every skill in it has run on live projects, under real deadlines, for real clients. The method case study on this site documents that work as it happens. What remains when the work is done is the intel: the tools and the judgment behind them, extracted and made usable by someone else.

01 — What it is

Around ten deep skills. Not forty shallow ones.

Each skill encodes a way of working that took years to earn. A voice codex that catches generic writing before it leaves the building. A counter-position that argues against the recommendation before the client does. A debrief that turns a finished project into something the next one can use. Plain language throughout. No dashboards, no personas, no setup theater.

Voice codex builder

Builds a written codex of how a brand actually sounds, then validates every draft against it. Generic phrasing gets flagged before anyone reads it.

Positioning sprint

A structured pass from raw client material to a defensible position. Built for the week when the strategy is due and the inputs are a mess.

Counter-position

Takes the recommendation on the table and builds the strongest case against it. The argument the client will make, made first, in private.

Reference library routing

Connects a maintained reference archive to live work. New material lands where the thinking already is, and resurfaces when a project needs it.

Project debrief

Closes out client work so the judgment survives the project. What held, what bent, what the next brief should know.

And the rest

The remaining skills are still earning their place on live work. They join the system when they have.
