A Roaring Twenties gala reopens the Ritz-Carlton Berlin.

For the grand reopening of the Ritz-Carlton Berlin, a Roaring Twenties-inspired Golden Gala was concepted and produced end to end: PR, artist programming, invitations, interior decoration, production and styling. Over 1,000 celebrities and VIPs attended, 500 bottles of champagne were poured, 7 artists performed.

The Ritz-Carlton Berlin is Marriott's signature address in Europe, the one the group points to. It had just come through a full interior renovation of every facility. The building was new again. The brand around it was not. The assets, the tone, the audience still read older than the rooms now did.

The work rebranded and redesigned every Berlin hospitality outlet across the property, restaurants and bars included, then put a Roaring Twenties Golden Gala on top of it to carry the relaunch. Built to set the property back in front of a younger, cultural Berlin, and to make the reopening an event people wanted to be at, not an announcement they received.

02 — Outlets Rebranding

Five spaces. One property. Each with its own identity.

For the Berlin location of the Ritz-Carlton, we redesigned the branding for five interior spaces: the Curtain Club piano bar, Fragrances cocktail bar, The Lounge, the Cigar Club, and POTS — a high-end restaurant helmed by Michelin star chef Dieter Müller. Each identity distinct, all complementary, rooted in the Art Deco interior and the history of Potsdamer Platz.

Curtain Club

An intimate piano bar with chic leather sofas and Art Deco styling. The identity mirrors the space's angles and shapes — a clean, elegant logo that would make Erté proud.

Fragrances

A bar concept that combines the world of perfumes with the art of cocktails. The identity takes influence from the geometric shapes of the space and the twinkling crystal of cocktail glasses and perfume bottles — a golden diamond icon, versatile and unmistakably luxurious.

The Lounge

The circular logo is inspired by the space's modernist light fixture and the different roles the Lounge plays throughout the day — from breakfast nook to afternoon tea to cocktail bar. A subtle blue hue reflects its adaptability.

Cigar Club

Black as the main colour, tone-on-tone approach. A bold and sleek statement — a logo that suggests a gentlemen's speakeasy of the Prohibition era.

POTS

Named and branded for the Ritz-Carlton. Chef Dieter Müller's experimental take on authentic German staples. The identity visualises the experience with a clean typeface and a playful signature — menus and coasters echo the restaurant's chrome accents.
