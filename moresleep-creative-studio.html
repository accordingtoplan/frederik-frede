MoreSleep

Identity · Digital · Campaign

"A new model for a sleepless industry."

A creative studio in Berlin and Los Angeles. Co-founded in 2012. Two founders and a core team of designers, developers and project managers, spread across time zones.

MoreSleep opened in Berlin in 2012. The founders had already built one agency, NoMoreSleep, and seen its team move into TBWA. Freunde von Freunden was running on the side.

The second studio started from a different question. How to make exemplary work and keep the hours sustainable. The name turned the old one around.

01 — History

From a Berlin garden to an Echo Park rooftop.

The founders met in Berlin in 2005. NoMoreSleep followed in 2006, a digital agency and production studio. Freunde von Freunden started in 2009 as a side project.

In 2010 TBWA acquired NoMoreSleep. That story has its own page. MoreSleep opened in 2012. Friends Space opened in 2016. A Kreuzberg office followed in 2017, a Los Angeles office in Echo Park in 2020.

02 — Offer

Strategy first. Then everything it needs.

Strategy and narrative. Concept. Brand transformation. Digital design and development. Content. Campaigns.

Design and engineering in one team. SPOT, an in-house asset management system, grew out of years of client builds. Collaborations and equity models sit next to classic client work.

03 — Values

Five values, each given an object.

Trust. Nobody knows everything, so talent gets pooled. Time. Used well, saved where possible. Curiosity. Questions lead to better ways of working.

Lasting. Beauty is good, usefulness lasts longer. Fun. Work worth doing can be fun to do. Each value got its own carrier: a flag, a cap, a newspaper, a laptop screen, a wrist.

04 — Culture

An office in Kreuzberg, one in Echo Park, many kitchen tables.

Remote from the first day. A team spread across cities and time zones, meeting in Berlin and Los Angeles. Self-starters who run their own projects and clients.

Projects stay open for input from anyone in the studio. Unlimited holidays. The best lines from the team chat ended up as a sticker set.

05 — In-house

Briefs from inside the studio.

Summer 2019 marked the tenth summer party, shared with Friends of Friends. The give-away shirt set the two studios in two typefaces, with date, time and place printed on the back.

The 2019 Christmas present to the team was a shirt with wishes for 2020. More fun, more time, more friends, more music, more books, more freedom.
