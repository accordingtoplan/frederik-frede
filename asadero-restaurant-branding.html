Asadero

Identity · Digital

"An empowering identity for the new grill in town."

Identity, art direction and website for Asadero, a sustainable parrilla from the Mezcla group.

Asadero is the latest restaurant from Mezcla. A sustainable grill with a twist on the classic Argentinian parrilla, where select cuts of meat cook on a spit called an asador.

The identity sets out to disrupt history. It inverts the gender roles around the gaucho, a figure traditionally cast as male.

01 — Logotype

Heavy serifs, high contrast.

A tailored logotype with thick geometric serifs and strong contrast. It carries the brutalist architecture and interior of the restaurant into type.

02 — Touchpoints

Card, clay and leather.

The logotype runs across business cards, packaging, flyers and tableware. Printed in red and orange, pressed into paper, clay and leather.

03 — Website

The same red, on screen.

The website keeps the red and the logotype of the restaurant. Five sections: about, menu, sustainability, reservations, contact.

Identity, art direction and website for Asadero, part of the Mezcla group. One logotype, drawn from the building, on stationery, tableware and screen.
