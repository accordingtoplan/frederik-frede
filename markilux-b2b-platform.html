B2B platform design for Markilux, the award-winning awning company. A central hub for global retailers to download assets, manage orders, and access marketing material and replacement parts.

Markilux Pro is the B2B area of Markilux, combining thousands of media files, marketing material, and a shop for replacement parts into a single platform for their global retailer network.

Designed to be simple and flexible. Backend powered by SPOT, the custom asset management solution built with ENGN, a newer, more considered approach to managing and distributing files at scale.
